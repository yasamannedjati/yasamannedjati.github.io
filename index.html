Yasaman Nedjati (she/her) is a Berlin-based set and spatial designer working across theatre, film, exhibition design, and architecture.

Her professional experience spans Berlin, Milan, Brussels, and Tehran. She has worked in theatre, exhibition design, film, and interior architecture across a range of projects and scales.

She studied Architecture at the University of Tehran, Interior and Spatial Design at Politecnico di Milano, and Scenography at ESA Saint-Luc Brussels.

Her work explores the relationship between people and space through narrative and scenography. Alongside her design practice, her research focuses on spatial conditions that allow for observation, solitude, and shifting relationships between what is onstage and offstage.
